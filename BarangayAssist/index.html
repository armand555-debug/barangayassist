Barangay Services

Barangay Clearances

Barangay Clearance

Request your essential local certificate online.

Request Service

Proof of good standing in the barangay, commonly needed for employment, school or other applications. Bring a valid ID and proof of residency. Requirements and fees may vary, so please confirm with the Barangay Hall.

Certificate of Residency

Confirm that you are a resident of the barangay.

Request Service

Used when you need to show proof of address, such as for school, bank or government transactions. Bring a valid ID and proof of residency. Requirements and fees may vary, so please confirm with the Barangay Hall.

Certificate of Indigency

Certify your status as a low-income resident.

Request Service

Commonly used to apply for assistance such as medical or scholarship aid. Bring a valid ID and proof of residency. Requirements and fees may vary, so please confirm with the Barangay Hall.

Barangay Business Permit / Clearance

Get the barangay clearance for your business.

Request Service

Required before applying for a city or municipal business permit. Bring a valid ID and proof of residency. Requirements and fees may vary, so please confirm with the Barangay Hall.

Certificate of Good Moral Character

Request proof of good moral standing.

Request Service

Often asked for by schools and employers. Bring a valid ID and proof of residency. Requirements and fees may vary, so please confirm with the Barangay Hall.

First-Time Jobseeker Certification

For first-time job applicants.

Request Service

Certifies your first-time jobseeker status for your employment papers. Bring a valid ID and proof of residency. Requirements and fees may vary, so please confirm with the Barangay Hall.
